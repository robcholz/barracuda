Barracuda serves people at every level of the ladder. Three groups matter most:
developers, education, and educational development-board companies.

## Developers

Developers are the core audience. They span the whole ladder.

| Who | Ladder | Wants | Barracuda gives them |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Makers and hobbyists | 1–2 | A smart device for a project without fighting toolchains | Browser install, an on-board agent that drives the hardware, a firmware with only the Plugins they choose |
| App developers | 3 | Real device apps with a UI, built fast | The Live Editor and the in-browser agent, flashing from the browser |
| Plugin developers | 4 | To add a capability once and have it work everywhere | The Plugin contract and the `create-plugin` skill in their own coding agent |
| Contributors | 5 | To bring Barracuda to the hardware they care about | `create-board` and `create-platform` |
| Builders | 6 | Product firmware sized to a chip and a job | `fully-customize`, and a runtime where everything is a removable Plugin |

What developers value most: a modern development experience on embedded, agents
that understand the codebase, and the freedom to go as deep as they want without
leaving the platform.

## Education

Schools, universities, training programs, and makerspaces.

- **Day one without setup.** Students install from the browser on school
  computers; there is no toolchain to manage per seat.
- **From words to hardware.** Students first ask the board, in plain language,
  to do something physical — and see the script the agent wrote to do it.
- **From scripts to systems.** The ladder doubles as a curriculum: scripts,
  configuration, apps, Plugins, and finally boards and platforms. Each level
  teaches a real layer of an embedded system.
- **Real hardware, real code.** Everything students see is real: the scripts, the
  apps, the Plugins, the source.

## Educational development-board companies

Companies that design and sell development boards and kits for learning and
making.

- **Their board, ready for AI.** A board added with `create-board` appears in the
  Web Installer; customers install Barracuda onto it from a browser.
- **Their hardware, exposed to agents.** On-board peripherals become VM packages
  and Plugins, so the on-board agent, apps, and scripts can use everything on the
  board.
- **A curriculum to sell with the kit.** The ladder gives kits a path from first
  lesson to advanced project.
